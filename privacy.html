Soccer Trail — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Soccer Trail ("the app") is an Android app that tracks a player in a video you choose and draws their running trail. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app itself handles

  • Your videos stay on your device. The app reads the videos you pick, tracks the player, merges videos and exports the result entirely on your phone. Videos, music you add to an export, tracking edits and save files (.stp) are never uploaded by the app.
  • No account, no sign-in. The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal information.
  • Local activity log. To help diagnose problems, the app keeps a log of your actions inside its own storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere; you can share it with us yourself if you report a bug.
  • No media permissions. The app cannot browse your photo, video or music library. It only reads the videos and music you pick yourself in the system picker, and keeps access to those so you can continue your last edit.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID,
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, IP address used to estimate approximate location),
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (such as crashes and performance).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy button on the app's start screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
